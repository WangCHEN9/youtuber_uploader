Privacy Policy

ytupload · last updated 27 September 2026

ytupload is a local command-line tool, not a hosted service. It runs entirely on the computer of the person using it. There is no server, no database and no operator with access to your data.

1. What the application accesses

With your permission, ytupload uses the YouTube Data API v3 under a single OAuth scope, youtube.force-ssl. It uses that access only to:

  • upload video files to your own YouTube channel;
  • set the title, description, tags, category and privacy status of those uploads;
  • set a custom thumbnail on those uploads;
  • read your playlist titles, and add your uploads to one of your playlists;
  • read your channel name, in order to confirm which account was authorised.

It does not read, modify or delete videos it did not upload, and it does not access analytics, comments, subscribers or any other account data.

2. What is stored, and where

Everything is stored on your own computer. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

  • token_youtube_v3.json — the OAuth credentials issued by Google after you authorise the application. This file grants access to your account; keep it private.
  • secret/ — the OAuth client credentials you downloaded from Google Cloud Console.
  • uploaded_files.txt — a list of video file paths already uploaded, so the same file is not uploaded twice.
  • .upload_sessions.json — resumable upload session identifiers, so an interrupted upload can continue.

3. What is transmitted

The only data sent anywhere is what you explicitly choose to upload: your video file and the metadata you supply, sent to YouTube over HTTPS. Thumbnail artwork and hero images may be downloaded from Valve's public content delivery network.

No data is transmitted to the author of this application or to any third party. There is no analytics, no telemetry, no tracking and no advertising.

4. Sharing

Your data is never sold, rented or shared. The author of this application has no access to it, because it never leaves your computer.

5. Retention and deletion

Because all data is local, you control retention entirely. To remove the application's stored data, delete the files listed in section 2. To revoke its access to your Google account, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove this application. Revoking access immediately invalidates any stored credentials.

6. Google and YouTube

This application uses YouTube API Services. Your use of it is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Children

This is a developer tool and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.

8. Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date. The full revision history is public in the repository.

9. Contact
